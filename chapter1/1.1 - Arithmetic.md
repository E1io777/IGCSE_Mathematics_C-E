# 1.1 Arithmetic

## 1.1.1 Inequality & Comparison Symbols
### Concept Overview
Comparison symbols express the relationship between two quantities:

- **`=` (Equal to):** Both sides have the exact same value.
- **`≠` (Not equal to):** Both sides are not equal.
- **`<` (Less than):** The left side is smaller than the right side.
- **`>` (Greater than):** The left side is larger than the right side.
- **`≤` (Less than or equal to):** The left side is smaller than or equal to the right side.
- **`≥` (Greater than or equal to):** The left side is larger than or equal to the right side.

```{tip}
The open mouth of the symbol (`<` or `>`) always "eats" the larger number!
```
