## 1.1.1 Inequality & Comparison Symbols
### Concept Overview
Comparison symbols express the relationship between two quantities:

- **`=` (Equal to):** Both sides have the exact same value.
- **`≠` (Not equal to):** Both sides are not equal.
- **`<` (Less than):** The left side is smaller than the right side.
- **`>` (Greater than):** The left side is larger than the right side.
- **`≤` (Less than or equal to):** The left side is smaller than or equal to the right side.
- **`≥` (Greater than or equal to):** The left side is larger than or equal to the right side.

```{tip}
The open mouth of the symbol (`<` or `>`) always "eats" the larger number!
```

## 🎯 Try It Yourself

Compare the two numbers.

**8 ? 12**

Choose the correct symbol:

:::{pyodide-cell}
:id: inequality-question-1

import ipywidgets as widgets
from IPython.display import display

question = widgets.HTML(
    value="<h3>What symbol makes this statement correct?</h3>"
)

buttons = widgets.ToggleButtons(
    options=["<", ">", "="],
    description="Answer:"
)

feedback = widgets.HTML()

def check_answer(change):
    if change["name"] == "value" and change["new"] is not None:
        if change["new"] == "<":
            feedback.value = "<p style='color: green;'><b>✅ Correct!</b> 8 is less than 12.</p>"
        else:
            feedback.value = "<p style='color: red;'><b>❌ Not quite.</b> 8 is smaller than 12, so the correct symbol is &lt;.</p>"

buttons.observe(check_answer)

display(question)
display(widgets.HTML("<h2 style='text-align:center;'>8&nbsp;&nbsp;?&nbsp;&nbsp;12</h2>"))
display(buttons)
display(feedback)
:::
