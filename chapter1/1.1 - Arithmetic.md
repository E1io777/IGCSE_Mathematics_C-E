# 1.1 Arithmetic

## 1. Inequality & Comparison Symbols
- **`=`**: Equal to
- **`≠`**: Not equal to
- **`<`**: Less than
- **`>`**: Greater than
- **`≤`**: Less than or equal to
- **`≥`**: Greater than or equal to
- ### Examples
- $0.7 < \frac{7}{9}$
- $\frac{3}{4} = 0.75$

---
## 2. Decimals & Operations

### Key Rules
- **Multiplication:** Multiply as whole numbers, then count the total decimal places from both numbers to place the decimal point in the final answer.
- **Division:** Multiply both the dividend and divisor by $10, 100,$ or $1000$ to turn the divisor into a whole number before dividing.

### Worked Example
$$1.2 \times 0.04 = 0.048$$
